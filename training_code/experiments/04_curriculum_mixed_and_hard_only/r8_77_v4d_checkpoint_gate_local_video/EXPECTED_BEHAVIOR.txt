R8.77 selected V4D checkpoint: expected local-video phenomena

Reset contract
  The video must log R876_EVAL_DISTRIBUTION phase=full_support. Robot and ball
  are sampled independently across the playable interior and robot yaw spans
  the full [-pi, pi] range. This is not an easy curriculum render.

Expected successful behavior
  1. Find/turn: when initially facing away, Go2 should make a deliberate turn
     and bring the gray ball into its forward working direction. A short stand
     decision is acceptable; remaining stationary for an entire episode is not.
  2. Approach: distance to the ball should generally decrease without large,
     repeated left-right corrections. Backward or lateral motion is acceptable
     when it is clearly part of reorientation.
  3. Push selection: push should become dominant only near the ball. From far
     away, locomotion/turning should dominate rather than blind pushing.
  4. Ball progress: after contact, the ball should move toward the opponent goal
     often enough to produce positive progress and occasional goals. Contact
     alone is not success if the ball is driven sideways into a wall.
  5. Wall behavior: near either side wall or a corner, Go2 should turn away,
     reposition, or leave the wall. Repeated collision, edge pinning and rapid
     twitching are failures.
  6. Low-level quality: the torso should remain upright, feet should execute a
     recognizable gait, and joint motion should remain smooth. The expected
     metric range is upright error well below 0.03 and no persistent action-rate
     oscillation.
  7. Symmetry: left- and right-side situations should not show a clear one-sided
     collapse. The deterministic metric gate, not one attractive clip, decides
     this property.

Expected reset transitions
  A new state may appear immediately after a goal, own goal, base termination,
  or episode timeout. Teleportation at such a reset boundary is normal. Sudden
  teleportation mid-episode without a termination is abnormal.

Reject on sight
  - ignores the gray ball or walks consistently away from it;
  - stays still for a full episode despite a reachable ball;
  - pins itself to a wall and twitches repeatedly;
  - repeatedly circles the ball without closing distance;
  - pushes primarily toward its own goal;
  - visibly unstable or malformed Delta Pose gait;
  - video log does not contain the full-support distribution marker.

Interpretation limit
  This video is qualitative evidence for the checkpoint already selected by
  the 13-case R8.75 metric gate. It must not replace the metric comparison and
  must not be described as a statistical full-support success rate.
